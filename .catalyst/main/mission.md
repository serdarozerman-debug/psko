# Mission: PSKO

## Vision

Bridge the gap between theoretical psychology education and real-world clinical practice by giving every psychology student access to unlimited, safe, evidence-based patient simulations — powered by AI.

## Problem

Psychology students face a critical "theory-practice gap":
- Supervised clinical hours are scarce and competitive
- Role-play with peers is unrealistic (peers avoid challenging the student, relationships create bias)
- Real patient exposure too early raises ethical concerns and increases student anxiety
- Students graduate under-prepared for the complexity and unpredictability of actual clinical interactions

## Solution

PSKO is an AI-powered clinical psychology simulation platform where:
- Students practice as the therapist with AI-generated patient personas
- Each persona has a realistic cognitive model, emotional state, disorder profile, and conversational style
- The system guides students in real-time: what to ask, when to pivot, how to apply a therapeutic framework
- After each session, an AI supervisor provides structured competency-based feedback
- All major therapeutic approaches are covered: CBT, Psychodynamic, Humanistic/Person-Centered, ACT, DBT

## Target Users

**Primary:** Psychology students (undergraduate and graduate) practicing clinical interview and therapy skills.

**Secondary:** Clinical psychology educators looking for scalable, standardized practice tools for their programs.

## Success Metrics

- Students can independently conduct a structured intake interview after 5 simulation sessions
- Students correctly identify the appropriate therapeutic approach for a given persona profile
- Students demonstrate improved competency scores (CTS-R equivalent) over time
- Platform used by 1,000+ students within 12 months of launch

## Core Principles

1. **Evidence-based** — All personas and feedback grounded in clinical competency frameworks (UCL CORE, CTS-R)
2. **Safe to fail** — Students must feel no real-world consequences when making mistakes
3. **Immediate feedback** — Feedback given after each session, not weeks later
4. **Multi-approach** — No single school of thought; students experience the full clinical toolkit
5. **Accessibility** — Web-first, no installation, works on any device
